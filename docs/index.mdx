Sodax is cross-chain execution infrastructure for modern DeFi. You integrate one SDK — `@sodax/sdk` — and your application can swap, lend, borrow, bridge, and stake across 21+ blockchain networks as if there were no boundaries between them. Sodax handles liquidity coordination, intent execution, and settlement so you can focus on building the experience your users need.
